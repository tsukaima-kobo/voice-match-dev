    しくみHow it works

    一言から。話すほど絞り込む。Start with a phrase. Talk more, get closer.

    1. 01

      一言話すSay a phrase

      セリフ 1 つでも、お題に答えても。ふだんの声でも、声真似でも。A single line or an answer to a prompt. Your usual voice or an impression.

    2. 02

      近い声が出るSee the closest voices

      この端末の中で声を数字にして、事前に測った声と比べ、近い順に 5 つ出します。音はすぐに捨てます。Your voice is turned into numbers on this device, compared with voices measured in advance, and the five closest are shown. The sound is thrown away at once.

    3. 03

      話すほど絞り込むTalk more to narrow it down

      もう一言ずつ話すと、数字をまとめて結果が確かになります。続けて長く話すと、速さや間まで 45 項目で比べます。Each extra phrase is combined with the earlier ones for a surer result. Talk longer in one go and tempo and pauses join in, up to all 45 measures.
